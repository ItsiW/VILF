One day you will find yourself visiting Ocean Beach with a friend, during an emotionally turbulent time. You will get the pleasant tram ride all the way out there until you reach the end of the world. You will approach the beach, and second guess your choices as the cold wind flaps your jacket around and you question the well-intentioned ocean dip. The questioning will win out, and you will linger for a bit, then retreat. You will walk, and see a highly gentrified wine bar that looks like one you've seen a million times but is nice and done for a reason. You'll sit outside in the sun, there is no more wind. You'll eat a $23 **roasted cauliflower hoagie** and, while you normally like flavorful beers, be reminded that a lager is often correct and widely loved for a reason.
