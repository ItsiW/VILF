This is a chocolate place that makes expensive fancy chocolate and hot drinks. The best thing about it is that you get to **infinitely sample all the chocolates** while you wait in the line! And all the chocolate is vegan, just cacao and sugar. It's an adventure in the beans themselves, and they're very tasty.

The main thing to get here though are the **hot chocolates** and **mochas**. They can do most of them vegan if you just do it with an alt milk, just not the European drinking chocloate. I usually get the **Mexican Mocha** which is a spiced hot chocolate with a shot of espresso.

They also have a vegan carrot cake, I think other people like it but it tastes a bit generic vegan to me.

The drinks are a bit pricey, like $9 with tax and tip, but I think it's worth it with all the samples. The vibe inside is a little sterile so I like to grab a drink and go to Dolores Park.
