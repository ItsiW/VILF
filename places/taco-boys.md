Truly one of those places where you leave feeling far iller than when you entered. A heavyness and greasiness that's hard to beat.

They do have an extensive vegan menu. Probably way too many options. I thought one of the good things about being vegan is that it took away choice paralysis. Not here. Not that the dishes are very different, you just get way too many choices for the meat to put in.

It's probably best treated as a way to try different fake meats so you know what to buy at home. So get tacos, and not the chimichanga my companion opposite got. Insane.

The Beyond steak ends are fkn great!
