Yummy yummy ice cream. They always have 2-4 vegan flavors on the go. They've got this **ube coconut** flavor that's awesome, the **thai tea** one is good too.

I know what you're thinking, you want to get that coconut condensed milk drizzle. Don't do it, it's bad.

$8-9 for a double scoop. Share with a pal, good value.
