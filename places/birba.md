We came to this wine bar before the symphony, in a very pre-symphony wine-drinking mood.

Come for the wines, not the vegan foods. It's mostly a tinned fish place, with some other tinned things. Our pickled veg plate was almost entirely pickled carrot. Nothing wrong with that, but I'm not going to recommend for you to come here for that. Good for snacks to go with wine though.
