Are you hung over? Do you need an old-timey diner with unlimited coffee refills? St. Francis, among many other thing,  is the patron saint of stowaways. So check out what you can stow away in that big gob of yours.

The **seitan philly** is very good, it would benefit from sauerkraut but this was very tasty. Get it on rye. We also got the **vegan mess** which is a tofu scramble thing, would probably be better without the vegan cheese. 

About $30 each after tips and tax including coffee and orange juice and I think guacamole but I can't quite remember.
