It has been mentioned to me that sometimes I just like warm comforting stewy food on cold days. But so what, maybe that's all you need. Maybe that's good. Have you thought of that?

This place has **tofu stew** like a warm hug. The **mung bean pancakes** were also very nice. You get a ton of simple but tasty sides included.

$40 with tips and tax for all of this. Slightly less than enough food for two people.
