Great new Himalayan place on Valencia St. It's super cheap, each dish is under $15 and they're big. The **chili momos** were awesome, deep fried and saucy. Could be hotter though. The jhol momos (steamed in suace) were a little bland. And we got a **thali plate** that they could make vegan, which had 5 dishes of which 3 were great. And that was just $16 which is crazy these days.

Great place! Go here.
