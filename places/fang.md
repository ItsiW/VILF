This Chinese SF institution is truly an institution. Not one with amazing vegan options, but nonetheless interesting for a big group.

You don't really order things, you just give your dietary requirements and they bring a bunch of things out on ceremonial serving plates.

It's the same food that's at [House of Nanking](/places/house-of-nanking), so the mustard tofu is pretty tasty... everything else was very mid. I can't remember how much it was but it was definitely too much food and not the cheapest.
