I normally don't leave reviews for burger restaurants, because they are for children. But I will admit that this was a very efficient way of delivering a dose of saturated fat to my arteries to remind my heart what it means to live.

Everything here is vegan, very unfortunate because I was weighed down by choice paralysis. I went for the **Maverick Burger** because it had the most things on it. Around $20 after tips and tax. So I guess pricy for a burger? I'm not sure, I don't eat burgers.

A pro tip I heard was to ask for the soft serve that they normally do for the smoothies. They have coconut and soy, make sure to get the **soy soft serve**, it is far better, I tried both. Genuinely the best soft serve that exists.
