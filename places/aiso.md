A new bar for the vegans. It is extremely cute and well executed.

We got a few things, the stand out was clearly the **garlic butter udon**. The platonic ideal of garlic and butter.

The other things we were less enthusiastic about, but they were nice. But we didn't try everything! Go and explore. 

We highly recommend the **soggy dollar** cocktail as well.
