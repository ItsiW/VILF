I know what you're thinking, Ethiopian food dive bar? What could go wrong? 

The answer: nothing really. It turns out even when Ethiopian food isn't the best, it's still pretty good. Get the **vegetarian combo plate**.

Food and a drink is like $35 a head including tip and tax. Not great, not terrible.

And you know what, given that Nopa is a food desert, this ain't too bad.
