Very expensive South Indian place that I would only go to if someone else is paying. Lucky for me, they were.

A few things were a bit meh, like the pani puri and the dumplings a few of the other appetizers. The **mains** were good, but I'm forgetting what they were. Not like way better than a lot of the other upscale Indian places though, I'd probably rather go to [Copra](/places/copra). But I'd defnitely eat here again.

Also, the drinks weren't amazing. They were damn strong though.

We got a lot of food and 2 drinks each and it ended up being $150 a head, so it's pricey.
