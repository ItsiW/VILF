We had a wonderful deep Tenderloin Valentine's Day dinner at Azalina's. The security guard was really friendly, and the closed blinds to the outside had a certain charm.

The place is really lovely, and it's great they do a vegan tasting menu. A couple of the dishes were really good (they change them all the time so there's no point listing them). It was an unfortunate flow because the earlier dishes were a lot better than the later ones.  

Could be that it's a smashing place, they change it up all the time. But maybe a bit risky for the hefty price tag, it was $275 for two of us, a menu and a drink each (including tax and tip).
