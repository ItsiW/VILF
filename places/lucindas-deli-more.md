Good sandwich! Come here for a good sandwich. They've only got one vegan sandwich, it's the **avo smash**, it's got a good amount of red sauce and pickles and crunchy things. Too much avo for me to truly love it but you'll probably like it. But like $20 including tip and tax, so an expensive good sandwich. 

They share a space with Third Wheel Coffee, which also has the best coffee in the area. Check it out!
