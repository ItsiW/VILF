Original photos are copied separately to ~/Backups/vilf/photos. Only new or changed originals are downloaded; checksums are verified. Generated thumbnails are not copied.

Photo copies keep their latest backed-up version, with no automatic deletion. Replaced versions are retained in the cloud backup for 30 days, not in this Mac photo folder.
